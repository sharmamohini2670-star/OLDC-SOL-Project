Why Partner With OLDC?

Academic Trust & Legacy

Backed by the reputation and academic excellence of the University of Delhi. Strong governance, transparent processes, and academic integrity at core.

Innovation in Open Learning

Modern digital learning models, flexible formats, and scalable delivery frameworks. From content development to EdTech integration built for future-ready learners.

Collaborative Growth

Co-creation of programs, resources, and skill-building pathways. Mutual value generation through shared expertise and aligned objectives.

With the strength of Delhi University’s academic legacy and the agility of open learning, every partnership at OLDC becomes an opportunity to shape new narratives in education.
Deekshant Awasthi, Academic Coordinator
